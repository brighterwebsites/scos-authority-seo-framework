## **Service Page Sections**

**Standard service pages should include:**

1. **Hero/Opening** \- Problem statement \+ outcome promise  
2. **What's Included** \- Detailed scope  
3. **How It Works** \- Process/timeline  
4. **Proof Section** \- Relevant claims from Proof Library (2-4 claims)  
5. **Who It's For** \- Target client characteristics  
6. **Pricing** \- Transparent ranges or starting prices  
7. **FAQ** \- 5-8 common questions  
8. **Next Steps** \- Clear CTA (multiple options)
