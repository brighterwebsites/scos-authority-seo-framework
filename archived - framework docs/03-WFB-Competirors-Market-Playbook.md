# Competitors 🤼

# **Overview**

This process ensures a complete competitor landscape review before moving into personas, brand voice and marketing strategy development. It captures a broad initial set of competitors, narrows down to those most relevant, and gathers deep insights that inform positioning, content, and marketing objectives.

This step is focused on discovering the **business’s real competitors** — both those they know about (direct) and those dominating the digital space (digital competitors). The goal is to build a clear, accurate list of who you are truly competing with for your customers’ attention, leads, and sales.

**Approach:**

* **Direct Competitors:**  
  * Ask the business who they typically compete with or get compared to when quoting jobs, pitching services, or talking with prospects.  
  * Include businesses offering the same products/services to the same target audience.  
* **Digital Competitors:**  
  * Research who is ranking on Google for the main keywords the business wants to target.  
  * Identify who is running ads in search and social for similar offerings.  
  * Look for strong content publishers, directories, or platforms that dominate search results and social conversations in the space.

**Note:** At this stage, we **only record direct and digital competitors.** Indirect competitors (DIY options, alternative solutions, or substitute products) will be captured in the competitor analysis stage, where we explore wider market threats and opportunities.

**Outcome:**  
 A **competitor list** that is:Focused on direct and digital rivals Ready for deeper analysis (USP, social proof, SEO performance, etc.) Grounded in both the business’s perspective and real digital market activity  This becomes the foundation and helps build the **Competitor Analysis**, where we go deeper into comparing positioning, content strategy, reviews, backlinks, and other digital metrics.
