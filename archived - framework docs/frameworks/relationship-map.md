Voice gaps work on intent, not keyword volume, because people searching for something unfamiliar don't know the right words yet. They describe the problem, the feeling, the situation — not the solution. AI infers the underlying intent and surfaces what it can actually answer confidently. So topical coverage that matches how people describe problems — not just what they search for — gets retrieved.
The extension you're describing is that if you're the only source answering a specific question in a specific context (Ballarat, AI SEO, small business), AI doesn't just cite you — it potentially uses your framing to generate the next suggested question. Which means you're not just answering the conversation, you're shaping it.
That's a compounding authority loop:

You cover the topic deeply with local/contextual specificity
AI retrieves you because you're the credible available answer
AI suggests follow-up questions based on what it can answer confidently
Those questions lead back to your content
Which reinforces your citation frequency

The part that makes this more than theory is that it's already observable in how AI handles niche or geographically specific queries — it doesn't hallucinate answers it can't support, it surfaces what exists and builds the conversation thread around available authority.
The ALTC framework is already positioned for this — voice gaps + topical depth + local specificity is almost exactly the input profile that maximises AI retrieval and citation in a defined context.
